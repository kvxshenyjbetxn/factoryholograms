**Factory Holograms** lets you see at a glance what each machine in your GTNH base does. Look at a machine and press **Shift+H** to give it a label and add icons for its inputs and outputs. When an output on one label matches an input on another, the mod draws a line between them so you can follow the production chain right in the world.

Only you can see your labels. No server installation is required.

*You can customize the label colors, opacity, and render distance. If the lines get in the way, press **L** to hide them without hiding the labels.*
